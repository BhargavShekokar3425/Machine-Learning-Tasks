### Big Mart Sales Prediction 🛒
Predict sales for products across different outlets using linear regression.

- **Algorithm**: `Linear Regression`
- **Dataset**: Historical sales data
- **Objective**: Predict sales based on store and product attributes.

### Car Price Prediction 🚗
Predict car prices based on various features such as age, brand, and mileage.

- **Algorithm**: `Multiple Linear Regression`
- **Dataset**: Car listings data
- **Objective**: Estimate the price of a car based on its characteristics.

### Cluster Segmentation using K-Means 📊
Segment customers into distinct groups based on purchasing behavior using K-Means clustering.

- **Algorithm**: `K-Means Clustering`
- **Dataset**: Customer purchase data
- **Objective**: Segment customers into clusters for targeted marketing.

### Credit Card Fraud Detection 💳
Detect fraudulent credit card transactions using classification models.

- **Algorithm**: `Random Forest, Logistic Regression`
- **Dataset**: Credit card transactions dataset
- **Objective**: Classify transactions as fraudulent or non-fraudulent.

### Diabetes Prediction 💉
Predict whether a patient has diabetes based on medical diagnostic data.

- **Algorithm**: `Logistic Regression`
- **Dataset**: Medical diagnostic records
- **Objective**: Predict diabetes based on medical factors.

### Fake News Prediction 📰
Detect fake news using natural language processing and machine learning classification techniques.

- **Algorithm**: `Naive Bayes, Random Forest`
- **Dataset**: News articles dataset
- **Objective**: Predict whether the news article is fake or real.

### Gold Price Prediction 📈
Predict the future price of gold based on historical market data.

- **Algorithm**: `Linear Regression`
- **Dataset**: Historical gold price data
- **Objective**: Predict future gold prices.

### Heart Disease Prediction ❤️
Predict the presence of heart disease based on various health metrics.

- **Algorithm**: `Decision Tree, KNN`
- **Dataset**: Heart disease patient records
- **Objective**: Classify patients as having or not having heart disease.

### House Price Predictor 🏡
Predict the price of houses based on various features such as size, location, and year built.

- **Algorithm**: `Multiple Linear Regression`
- **Dataset**: Real estate data
- **Objective**: Predict the house price based on given attributes.

### Loan Status Prediction 💸
Predict whether a loan applicant will be approved or denied based on financial and personal information.

- **Algorithm**: `Logistic Regression, SVM`
- **Dataset**: Loan application data
- **Objective**: Predict the loan approval status.

### Medical Insurance Cost Prediction 🏥
Predict the cost of medical insurance based on a person's health and lifestyle factors.

- **Algorithm**: `Linear Regression`
- **Dataset**: Insurance claims data
- **Objective**: Estimate medical insurance cost.

### Parkinson's Disease Detection 🧠
Detect Parkinson’s disease in patients based on speech signal features.

- **Algorithm**: `Support Vector Machine (SVM)`
- **Dataset**: Parkinson’s voice recording dataset
- **Objective**: Predict the presence of Parkinson’s disease.

### Sonar Mine Detection ⛏️
Classify sonar signals as either rocks or mines using a classification algorithm.

- **Algorithm**: `K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)`
- **Dataset**: Sonar signals dataset
- **Objective**: Classify sonar signals to detect underwater mines.

### Titanic Survival Prediction 🚢
Predict the survival chances of passengers aboard the Titanic based on their demographic information.

- **Algorithm**: `Logistic Regression`
- **Dataset**: Titanic passenger data
- **Objective**: Predict whether a passenger would survive the Titanic disaster.

### Wine Quality Predictor 🍷
Predict the quality of wine based on various chemical properties.

- **Algorithm**: `Random Forest, Decision Trees`
- **Dataset**: Wine quality dataset
- **Objective**: Predict the quality of wine based on chemical analysis.
